The Dual-Track Parallel Strategy (Phase 1)

To ensure continuous progress, decouple the complexity of dynamic movement from the complexity of force vector math by running two independent, parallel tracks.

🏃 Track 1: The Movement Engine (Dynamic STS)

- **Goal:** Achieve a flawless, error-free dynamic simulation of a human sitting down and standing up using the Full-Body Lumbar Spine (FBLS) model without any suit forces.
- **Key Inputs:** Raw motion capture data file (`.mot` or `.trc`).
- **Milestones to Complete:**
    1. **Kinematic Verification:** Ensure smooth joint trajectories (hip, knee, spine flexion) with zero joint dislocations or body-clipping bugs.
    2. **Inverse Dynamics (ID):** Compute the baseline net joint torques required to stand against gravity. Keep residual forces near zero.
    3. **Static Optimization (SO):** Generate unassisted baseline muscle activation curves (\(\sum a^2\)) for the primary lumbar extensors (`Erector Spinae`), tracking the peak at "seat-off."
- **Output:** Save these baseline curves into a `Baseline_No_Suit` control dataset.

🎯 Track 2: The Physics Lab (Static Force Testing)

- **Goal:** Master the syntax and implementation of external force vector injections within OpenSim without dealing with moving bodies.
- **Key Inputs:** FBLS model locked/frozen in a single, high-stress posture (e.g., a static 45-degree forward trunk lean).
- **Milestones to Complete:**
    1. **Actuator Selection:** Implement commercial strap configurations using the OpenSim `PathActuator` or `Ligament` classes attached to designated rigid bodies.
    2. **Vector Mapping:** Apply a constant, baseline external force vector (e.g., a 50N pull) and analyze how OpenSim handles line-of-action angles.
	    1. **Single-Frame Optimization Check:** Verify that the frozen lower back muscles instantly drop their force output when the external force is injected.
- **Output:** A verified, stable script template capable of altering anchor coordinates.

📊 Crucial Biomechanical Metrics to Monitor

To prove whether a specific strap location helps the user or actively causes harm, every parametric iteration must track and plot three key variables over the timeline:

- **\(F_{z}\) (Anterior-Posterior Shear Force):** _The Helper Vector._ Measures how well the suit pulls back on the torso to neutralize forward lumbar sliding caused by gravity.
- **\(F_{y}\) (Vertical Spinal Compression):** _The Hazard Vector._ Tracks if the vertical reaction force of the tight straps squeezes the **L4–L5** and **L5–S1** vertebrae past safe clinical limits (e.g., the NIOSH 3.4 kN threshold).
- **\(\sum a^2\) (Muscle Activation Drop):** Measures the percentage decrease in the `Erector Spinae` and `Multifidus` to prove real-world offloading.
